# Joran Dirk Greef / TigerBeetle

Reach for when:
- studying bounded-resource systems design
- studying static-memory and explicit-limit thinking
- studying deterministic simulation and assertion-heavy proof style
- studying APIs shaped by real OLTP and systems pressure

Transferable patterns:
- put limits on everything that matters
- let safety, perf, and operator predictability shape data and API design
- use assertions and simulation as force multipliers, not substitutes for thought
- keep first-principles cost reasoning tied to concrete workload

Watch:
- easiest to overfit extreme OLTP and safety pressure into everyday software
- some rules come from TigerBeetle's workload and fault model, not from Zig itself
