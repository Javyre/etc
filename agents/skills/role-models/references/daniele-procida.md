# Daniele Procida (Diátaxis)

Reach for when:
- choosing what kind of document a deliverable is before shaping it
- a brief or doc grows long because it mixes studying with deciding

Transferable patterns:
- four modes, each for a different need: tutorial (learning by doing),
  how-to (a task), reference (lookup while working), explanation (study)
- explanation serves study, not work, and "tends to absorb other things";
  keep it apart from the material a reader acts on

Watch:
- built for product documentation; a brief can combine modes when it keeps
  the decision material first and the study material behind it
