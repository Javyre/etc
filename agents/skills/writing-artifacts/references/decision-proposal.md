# Decision proposal

Primary audience: the person with authority to choose. Secondary audience:
reviewers supplying evidence or objections.

Purpose: make a consequential choice explicit and reviewable.

Include:

- decision requested and deadline when material
- problem and decision pressures
- viable alternatives and their consequences
- recommendation and value basis
- evidence, explicit inference, and open uncertainty
- follow-up contract after a decision

Complete when the decision owner can choose, reject, or request specific missing
evidence without reconstructing the problem.
