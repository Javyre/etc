# Decision record

Primary audience: the future maintainer.

Purpose: preserve a consequential choice and the forces that justify it.

Include:

- decision and current status
- problem and decision pressures
- alternatives considered and why they lost
- consequences, risks, and operational constraints
- evidence and explicit inference
- conditions that should reopen the decision

Complete when a future maintainer can apply the decision correctly and knows
when its rationale no longer holds.
